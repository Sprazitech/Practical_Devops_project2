## What I Learned

Through these tasks, I gained understanding of:

1. **Bare Metal Provisioning**: How physical servers are automated and managed like cloud resources
2. **Ironic Architecture**: The components, drivers, and workflows that enable bare metal deployment
3. **Bifrost Tool**: How Ansible playbooks can simplify complex infrastructure deployment
4. **OpenStack Ecosystem**: How services like Ironic fit into the broader infrastructure landscape
5. **Node History Importance**: Why tracking node state transitions and history is crucial for operators

## 📋 Your 4 Notebook Sections Explained Simply

### Section 1: Building an Article Worklist (1-2 hours)

**What you do:**
- Choose 10 Wikipedia articles on a topic you care about
- Answer 3 questions in markdown:
  1. List your 10 articles with reasons for choosing them
  2. Which is easiest to edit? (explain why)
  3. Which is most important? (explain why)

**Where to get help:**
- **NOTEBOOK_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md** Section 1 (has example answers)
- Just customize with your topic!

**Output:**
- Markdown cells with your article list and reasoning

---

### Section 2: Gathering Task Signals (3-4 hours)

**What you do:**
- Write 3 Python functions that score articles 0-1:
  1. `article_to_quality_feature_one()` → Citation quality
  2. `article_to_quality_feature_two()` → Structure quality
  3. `article_to_importance_score()` → Importance (pageviews)
- Write a paragraph justifying your choices

**Where to get help:**
- **NOTEBOOK_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md** Section 2 (complete code!)
- **microtask_generator_examples.py** (working functions)
- **QUICK_REFERENCE_CARD.md** (API syntax)

**Output:**
- 3 working Python functions
- Justification paragraph in markdown

---

### Section 3: Recommending Tasks (2-3 hours)

**What you do:**
- Run `worklist_to_recommended_tasks()` on your 10 articles
- Add timing measurements to track performance
- Answer reflection questions:
  - Does output match expectations?
  - Why is the slowest part slow?

**Where to get help:**
- **NOTEBOOK_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md** Section 3 (timing code)
- **OUTREACHY_MICROTASK_GUIDE.md** Phase 4

**Output:**
- Task recommendations for all 10 articles
- Timing analysis
- Reflection answers in markdown

---

### Section 4: Future Work and Reflection (2-3 hours)

**What you do:**
- Explain how to speed up the function (with code examples)
- Compare your tool to existing ones (Citation Hunt, PetScan, etc.)
- Explain what you'd adopt from each tool

**Where to get help:**
- **NOTEBOOK_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md** Section 4 (complete answers!)
- **OUTREACHY_MICROTASK_GUIDE.md** Phase 6

**Output:**
- Optimization strategies with code
- Tool comparison analysis
- Synthesis of best features
